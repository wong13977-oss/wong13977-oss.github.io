How do I check if a company sponsors work visas in the UK, US or Canada?

Updated 2026-10-14 · By Wong Xian Qi

To check whether a company sponsors work visas, look it up in each country's official register: the Home Office register of licensed sponsors (workers) in the UK, the US Department of Labor LCA disclosure data for H-1B, H-1B1 and E-3 filings, and Canada's positive LMIA employers list. All three are open government data that you can download and search yourself. The unofficial Visa Sponsor Checker API runs the same lookup for all three countries in one call.

How do I check if a company is a licensed sponsor?

  • UK: download the register from GOV.UK, a CSV of roughly 120,000 rows with columns such as Organisation Name, Town/City, County, Type & Rating and Route (one row per organisation and route). A listed employer holds a sponsor licence today; the register has no filing counts.
  • US: download the LCA disclosure XLSX files from the Department of Labor (hundreds of megabytes per year), filter by visa class and certified status, and count filings per employer.
  • Canada: open the Employment and Social Development Canada dataset on Open Canada and search the quarterly spreadsheet for the employer.

Read the counts carefully. A Labor Condition Application (LCA) is filed before an H-1B, H-1B1 or E-3 petition, so a certified LCA shows that an employer sponsors or intends to sponsor that visa; it is not a visa approval. A positive Canadian LMIA lets an employer hire a foreign worker for the listed positions.

Why is checking by hand slow?

Each country publishes its data differently (a CSV in the UK, XLSX files in the US and Canada), and listed names often differ from what you type. "Google" appears as "Google (UK) Limited" and "Google LLC"; "J.P. Morgan" appears as "JPMorgan Chase & Co.". Searching "Amazon" in the US data turns up several similar names, and the right one is "Amazon.com Services LLC" (81,822 filings in a sample run), not "Amazon LLC". The UK file also shows only who holds a licence now, so "first listed" dates need your own saved copies.

How do I check visa sponsors with an API?

You get one row per company and country with the status (matched or not_found), the employer name as published, matchConfidence from 0 to 1, sponsor routes, town, first and last seen dates, filingsCount, positionsCount and yearly history. FAQ

How do I check if a company is a licensed visa sponsor in the UK?

Search the Home Office register of licensed sponsors (workers) on GOV.UK for the organisation's name, or use the Visa Sponsor Checker API to look up many names at once. A listing means the organisation holds a sponsor licence today; the register shows routes such as Skilled Worker and a rating such as Worker (A rating), but no filing counts.

How can I look up an H-1B sponsor?

The US Department of Labor publishes LCA disclosure data as XLSX files, and you can count a company's certified LCAs for H-1B, H-1B1 and E-3. Visa Sponsor Checker does this for you and returns filings and positions per fiscal year since FY2021. A certified LCA is not a visa approval.

Where is the Canada LMIA employer list?

Employment and Social Development Canada publishes the positive LMIA employers list on Open Canada as a quarterly spreadsheet. Visa Sponsor Checker covers it from 2020 Q4 and returns approved LMIAs, positions and the stream, such as Global Talent Stream or High Wage.

Does "not found" mean a company never sponsors visas?

No. It means no matching employer is in that register for the period covered. The company may sponsor under a different legal name, may have applied before the covered period, or may use routes these registers do not list.

How often is the visa sponsor data updated?

The UK register is checked weekly, and Canada and the US are updated with every quarterly release. UK first-seen dates start on 2026-10-12, when the weekly snapshots began.

Is Visa Sponsor Checker an official government service?

No. It is an unofficial tool and is not affiliated with the UK Home Office, the US Department of Labor or Employment and Social Development Canada. It republishes their open data, so confirm important decisions on the official register or with an immigration adviser.
