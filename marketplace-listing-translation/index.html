How do you translate Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop listings within the title limits?

Updated 2026-10-14 · By Wong Xian Qi

Title limits differ by marketplace: the SEA Listing Localizer Actor applies 120 characters on Shopee (a common Seller Centre figure; the Actor's documentation says Shopee publishes no official number), 255 on Lazada and 25 to 255 on TikTok Shop. When you translate a title into Malay, Indonesian, Thai or Vietnamese, keep brand names, sizes and model codes exactly as written and check the result against the limit. Sellers in Malaysia must also show the seller information listed in the CPETTR 2024 Schedule, and the unofficial Apify Actor SEA Listing Localizer & CPETTR Checker translates, trims and checks listings through an API.

Why does machine translation of a product title go wrong?

A title packs product type, key specs and brand into few words, so literal translation breaks in predictable ways:

  • Wrong market vocabulary. Malaysian Malay and Indonesian differ in everyday words, such as "kereta" in Malaysia and "mobil" in Indonesia. The Actor keeps ms and id apart.
  • Changed protected terms. "750ml" must not become "750 mililiter", and a model code like "S24" or a brand name must survive. Sizes, capacities and model codes are protected automatically; add brand, model or ingredient names in protectedTerms. Terms missing from the result are listed in missingProtectedTerms (empty is good).
  • Keywords in the wrong order or language. The Actor puts the product type and key specs first, uses words local shoppers search for, and keeps English where shoppers commonly search in English, such as "power bank" or "serum".
  • Text marketplaces reject. Emoji and hashtags in titles, and links, phone numbers and e-mail addresses anywhere, are removed, and the AI is told to drop promotional phrases such as "FREE SHIPPING" or "BEST SELLER".

If a translated title is still over the limit, the Actor removes words from the end. The first word (the product type) and the protected terms stay; if even those do not fit, the title is cut mid-phrase and a note says so. Thai, which has no spaces between words, is trimmed at word or phrase boundaries. A shortened title has titleTrimmed: true.

What does Malaysia's CPETTR 2024 require sellers to disclose?

CPETTR is the Consumer Protection (Electronic Trade Transaction) Regulations 2024, P.U.(A) 449/2024, in force since 25 December 2024. The Actor's check follows its Schedule:

  • Seller or company name (item 1); e-mail address and telephone number (item 3); address of the place of business (item 4)
  • Main characteristics of the product (item 5); the Actor expects a description of at least 50 characters
  • Full price, and delivery cost or delivery terms (item 6)
  • Payment methods (item 7); terms and conditions, or at least the return and refund policy (item 8)
  • Estimated delivery time (item 9); a Bahasa Malaysia version of the information (Regulation 4)

Not scored but suggested: your website and any safety or health certificate (both "if any", items 2 and 10), and your company registration number, which marketplaces must keep on record (Regulation 8(1)(c)). The Actor's documentation says enforcement of the Bahasa Malaysia rule was postponed in June 2025.

The Actor turns this into a complianceScore (0 to 100) per listing, plus cpettrMissing and cpettrHints. In the CPETTR example run both listings scored 100. To read the rules, find the gazette text of P.U.(A) 449/2024; for legal questions ask a qualified adviser or the Ministry of Domestic Trade and Cost of Living (KPDN). The check is not legal advice.

FAQ

What is the Lazada product title character limit?

The SEA Listing Localizer Actor applies 255 characters for titles and 25,000 for descriptions on Lazada, taken from the Lazada Open Platform CreateProduct API. The Actor does not let you raise the Lazada limit.

How long can a TikTok Shop product title be?

The Actor applies 25 to 255 characters for titles and up to 10,000 for descriptions on TikTok Shop, based on the TikTok Shop Partner API. A title shorter than 25 characters gets a note asking you to add product details.

What is the Shopee product title limit?

According to the Actor's documentation, Shopee publishes no official limit, so the Actor uses 120 characters for titles and 3,000 for descriptions, the common Seller Centre figures. Check the number your Seller Centre shows for your category and set titleMaxLength (10 to 255) if it differs.

What must sellers disclose under Malaysia's CPETTR 2024?

The Actor's checklist follows the Schedule of P.U.(A) 449/2024: seller name, e-mail and telephone, business address, product description, full price and delivery cost, payment methods, terms and conditions, estimated delivery time, plus a Bahasa Malaysia version (Regulation 4, enforcement postponed in June 2025). It is a checklist, not legal advice.
